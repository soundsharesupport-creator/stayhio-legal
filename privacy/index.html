1. Core principle

Stayhio is a nearby social game. It is designed to process as much information as reasonably possible on your device and on nearby participants’ devices.

Stayhio does not require you to create a Stayhio account.

Stayhio does not sell personal data and does not use your content for advertising or cross-app tracking.

2. Local profile

On your device, Stayhio may store:

  • display name / nickname;
  • avatar choice (emoji or a custom image you add);
  • avatar color preferences;
  • local statistics such as sessions played, wins/survivals, losses, and best time;
  • Last Session result summary for quick reopen.

You may optionally take a profile photo with the camera. Camera access is requested only when you choose Camera for that purpose. Stayhio does not continuously access the camera.

This information stays on your device unless you join a session, in which case your display name and avatar presentation are shared with nearby participants for gameplay.

3. Nearby sessions and Local Network

Stayhio uses nearby-device discovery and local device-to-device networking (including Bonjour and local network connections) so people in the same place can create and join a session.

When you participate, Stayhio may exchange with nearby devices:

  • participant display name and avatar presentation;
  • ready / lobby state needed to start;
  • session configuration (for example participant count, calls allowed, loss sound choice, and optional custom punch or punishment text you enter);
  • gameplay events such as session start, loss claim, loss confirmation, rematch, and session end;
  • technical identifiers required to keep the session synchronized.

Local Network permission is requested so discovery and session communication can work. Connection quality depends on the devices, OS, and radio environment; Stayhio does not guarantee discovery or uninterrupted connectivity.

4. Gameplay and loss behavior

Stayhio is designed so participants can put their phones down and stay away from other apps during a session. Players lose when they leave Stayhio to use another app during an active session (for example Home, App Switcher, or opening another app).

Stayhio is designed to allow certain device and system states without treating them as a loss by themselves, including device lock, Auto-Lock / screen off, temporary system interruptions, and Control Center or Notification Center. When Calls Allowed is enabled for the session, Stayhio is designed to let participants receive and answer incoming system calls and return to the session. Manually leaving Stayhio to place or start a call in another app is still treated as leaving the app.

OS behavior cannot be guaranteed in every interruption case. Loss sounds may play on relevant devices. Custom punch or punishment text you enter is shown as configured for that session. Stayhio does not provide a built-in library of jokes or punches and does not generate punches from conversation audio in the current product.

5. Notifications

Stayhio may request notification permission so a losing player can be notified if the app is backgrounded. Notification content relates to the session result and may use a short loss sound where the system allows.

6. Stayhio Plus and StoreKit

If you subscribe to Stayhio Plus, purchase, renewal, cancellation, and Restore Purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit / App Store. Apple processes payment data under Apple’s terms and privacy policies. Stayhio receives entitlement status needed to reflect your subscription in the app. A limited entitlement cache may be stored in an App Group on your device to reflect Plus status.

7. Support messages

If you contact support from the app, the message you write is sent using your device’s mail or share sheet. Technical details you choose to include (for example app version) may be attached to help diagnose issues.

Support email: soundshare.support@gmail.com .

8. Apple services

Depending on features you use, Stayhio may rely on Apple frameworks and services such as Local Network / Bonjour networking, AVFoundation (for loss sounds and optional camera photos), StoreKit, and Notifications. Apple’s processing is governed by Apple’s applicable terms and privacy policies.

9. Retention and deletion

Local profile, statistics, Last Session, and developer overrides (if any) remain on your device until you clear them in-app (where available) or uninstall Stayhio. Uninstalling removes most app-local data from that device.

Stayhio also stores a small device-local counter used to track how many free hosted sessions have been used on the Free plan. That counter is kept in ordinary app storage and mirrored in the device Keychain so the Free plan usage limit can be preserved if the app is deleted and reinstalled. It is not an advertising identifier, is not used for tracking across apps, and is not synced through iCloud. It remains on the device until the Keychain item is cleared (for example by wiping the device or otherwise removing that Keychain data).

Nearby session data exists ephemerally among participating devices for the session and is not operated as a Stayhio cloud account store.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical measures appropriate for an on-device / nearby-device app. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.
