Stayhio is a nearby social game. A host can create a session; nearby people can join; participants stay in the app; the first participant to leave loses; the round ends. Optional features include loss sounds and a custom punch or punishment text that you write yourself for your own nearby session.

Stayhio does not provide a built-in library of jokes or punches and does not generate punches from conversation audio in the current product.
